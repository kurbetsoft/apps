Privacy Policy

Kakashkagram: Cringeverse (Android, package com.kurbetsoft.kakashkagram)
Developer: kurbetsoft
Effective date: November 26, 2026 (version 1.1.0, ads added)

Summary

We do not collect or store your personal data ourselves. The game has one optional ad: after losing, you can watch a video to continue. It is served by Google AdMob, and Google receives some device data to do so.

Game progress

Unlocked levels, score and sound settings are saved only on your device. We have no access to them. They are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads are shown only when you choose to: on the game over screen, with the "Watch an ad and continue" button. To load and show videos, the game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and connects to the internet. The SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • ad interactions: app launches, taps, video views;
  • diagnostics: launch time, hang rate, energy usage.

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit (TLS). We only receive aggregated reports from Google (impressions and revenue). How Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form (UMP) on first launch. You can refuse personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with the "Ad settings" button in the main menu.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings: Settings → Google → Ads (the path differs slightly between phones).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We ask Google to show no ads rated above PG and do not knowingly collect children's data.

Google Play services

Installation and updates are provided by Google Play. Google may collect app usage data under its own Privacy Policy. That data is not shared with us, except anonymous statistics in Play Console (such as install counts and crash reports).

Changes

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date above.
